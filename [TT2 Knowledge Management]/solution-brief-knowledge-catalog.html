Solution Brief: Knowledge Your AI Agents Can Actually Use

Turn scattered documents, data, and relationships — across every platform you already run — into a governed knowledge layer in BigQuery and Knowledge Catalog, so every question gets a correct, current, cited answer.

The Challenge

Enterprise AI agents are failing in production because they face an Enterprise Context Gap. High-value decisions — approving a loan, diagnosing a machine, rerouting a supply chain, or investigating complex cases — need both the live operational numbers in databases and the business rules locked in contracts, manuals, and tickets. Those two worlds are disconnected, and the knowledge itself was built for humans, not machines: unreadable in document layouts, unconnected across systems, and unexplained in meaning, ownership, and permissions. Agents therefore retrieve text that looks relevant rather than facts that are correct — unable to reason across documents, cite their sources, or tailor answers to who is asking. The bottleneck is no longer model capability. It is the missing governed knowledge layer between the data and the agent.

Why it's hard to solve What good looks like
The bottleneck has moved from model capability to context. And that context is spread across platforms you've deliberately invested in — so the answer can't be "consolidate everything first." Getting the right knowledge, with the right permissions, into the model at the right moment is a data engineering and governance problem, solved across your estate rather than by rebuilding it. Anyone — or any agent — asks a question in plain language and gets a correct, cited answer that respects their permissions. Including multi-hop questions that span an unstructured document, a resolved golden entity record, and an operational event log at the same time.

The Solution

A governed knowledge layer between your data and your agents. It reads content that already exists — no one has to rewrite documents — and reaches across your existing platforms rather than replacing them. Three jobs, not one search box:

  • Understand — read documents, calls, and images with layout-aware parsing (AI.PARSE_DOCUMENT) and autonomous embeddings (AI.EMBED), so structure, tables, and hierarchy survive extraction.
  • Connect — resolve entity aliases into canonical golden records, standardize relationship vocabularies (AI.CLASSIFY), and model how everything relates in BigQuery Graph (GRAPH_TABLE).
  • Serve — retrieve using BigQuery Hybrid Search (AI.SEARCH / Reciprocal Rank Fusion over dense vectors + lexical search index) enriched with BigQuery Knowledge Catalog business definitions and row-level permissions.
